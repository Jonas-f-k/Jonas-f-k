# Art direction — 0.6.0-rework.1

Broad Minecraft pixel clusters, readable anatomy and restrained material highlights. Charcoal, slate, warm metal, jade, turquoise, frost and pearl distinguish regions and Aspect families. Natural ground edges match; leaves use transparent pixels and native cutout rendering.

Every species owns a complete hierarchical rig, with details attached to the correct parent. Pincers move with forearms; teeth follow jaws; wing tips follow elbows; tail segments inherit the preceding segment's transform. Species with a shared biological family retain recognizable related anatomy while changing crests, proportions, plates, limbs and attack behavior.

Transformation anatomy: a horned dragon with independent forearms and wings; a four-legged wolf; a segmented leviathan with fins; a broad armored colossus; a hoofed stag with branching antlers; a four-legged feathered griffin with wings; a wyvern whose forelimbs form the wings. First-person form limbs remain available separately.

The eight illustrated atlas PNGs and their runtime importer are retired. Native sprites stitch through Minecraft's single-sprite source. Creature skin motifs distinguish fur, scale, bark, metal and feather-like surfaces. Equipment has broad 64px panels and trims. 128 Memory IDs resolve to different transparent icons. See `ASSET_CREDITS.md` for unchanged upstream assets.

Minecraft's normal entity lighting supplies directional face shading. Eyes and armor trims use separate emissive passes. The mod adds no replacement shader or framebuffer. Seven bounded spell-geometry families use the built-in translucent render path.

The supplied image references mentioned in the pasted goals were not attached in this turn. These are original voxel designs built from the written goals and the source foundation. The offline shaded contact sheet presents geometry for review; it does not prove final in-world lighting, animation appearance or performance.
